## What the specification covers

It documents the two distinct architectural namespaces—legacy `ore_n1`–`ore_n9` and canonical `F-A`–`F-J` / `B-N1`–`B-N9`—and explains why they must not be confused.

It also covers the 66 ORE packages, shared contracts and module registry, workspace coordination, memory and learning systems, semantic architectures, USG integration, experiment workbench, data provenance, API compatibility, testing, and scientific limitations.

Most importantly, it gives other AI models concrete working rules: inspect before changing code, preserve existing interfaces, keep inference separate from authorized learning, retain uncertainty and provenance, and never claim that a simulated biological process has been experimentally validated.
