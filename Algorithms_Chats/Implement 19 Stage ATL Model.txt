The implementation is available for review in **[PR #24 — Implement 19-stage amodal ATL semantic processing](https://github.com/wizekin/ORE-Neurocomputational/pull/24)**.

It adds the `ore_atl_amodal` package, with all 19 functional stages, a separate bilateral ATL LIF simulation, explicit mathematical-operation records, phrase-composition and denotation handling, and Hebbian learning with opt-in updates. ### Implementation structure

| Stages | Implemented functionality |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Sensory encoding, feature extraction, distributed spokes, normalization, and ATL transmission |
| 6–10 | Bilateral ATL integration, shared semantic representations, cross-modal generalization, feedback, and concept retrieval |
| 11–15 | Noun activation and maintenance, adjective processing, relational binding, and phrase composition |
| 16–18 | Subsective/privative denotation, temporal refinement, and unified expression meaning |
| 19 | Hebbian cross-modal learning with weight decay and authorization controls |

This follows the source's 19-stage sequence and preserves its distinction between proposed computational mechanisms and experimentally established neural findings. <FileCite ref_id="turn1file0" />
