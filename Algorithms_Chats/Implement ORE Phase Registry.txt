## 4. Important scientific boundary

- B-N2 through B-N8 have explicit backend integration boundaries, but their canonical biological implementations remain future work.
- Missing biological backends are reported as unavailable rather than simulated through misleading legacy aliases.
- Passing software tests does not establish biological validity.
