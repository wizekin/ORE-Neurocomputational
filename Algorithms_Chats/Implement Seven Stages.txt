The simulation implements membrane integration, spike thresholding, reset, refractory periods, synaptic currents, recurrent excitation, global inhibition and adaptation.

It reports:
- Membrane potential in mV.
- Synaptic current in pA.
- Spike counts and firing rates in Hz.
- Simulated time in milliseconds.

The biological model is a configurable computational reference, not an experimentally validated reconstruction of human reading circuitry. Its activity does not modify the functional posterior.
